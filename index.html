The science, simply

Why some foods are paired on purpose

Every lunch and dinner in the planner quietly follows these two rules, and tracks iron-rich foods across the week so they don't get crowded out.

IRON + VITAMIN C

Vitamin C helps iron absorb

Iron-rich foods — egg yolk, liver, minced meat, ndengu, lentils, beans — are absorbed far better alongside a vitamin-C food in the same meal, like spinach, tomato, or sukuma wiki. The planner aims for iron at lunch on most days of the week, not just whenever it happens to land.

FAT + VITAMIN A

Fat helps vitamin A absorb

Vitamin-A foods — pumpkin, butternut, carrot, sweet potato — are fat-soluble, meaning a spoon of avocado, natural yogurt or a little groundnut paste alongside them helps baby actually absorb the vitamin, not just taste it.

Responsive feeding

Signs baby is hungry

  • Opens mouth or moves toward the spoon
  • Smacks lips, sucks on fists or fingers
  • Gets alert or excited at the sight of food
  • Reaches or points at food (older babies)
  • Eats willingly and happily

Feed only when baby shows these cues — it gives time to digest and keeps mealtimes pressure-free.

Responsive feeding

Signs baby is full

  • Turns head away or closes mouth
  • Pushes the spoon or bowl away
  • Slows down or stops eating willingly
  • Plays with food instead of eating it
  • Becomes fussy or distracted

Never force the last spoonful — babies are good at regulating their own appetite.

Golden rules

Introducing new foods safely

  • Start with 1–2 tablespoons, once a day, for the first 2–3 days.
  • Introduce one new food at a time, and wait 2–3 days before the next — this makes it easy to spot any reaction.
  • From 6 months, breastmilk or formula alone is no longer enough iron — prioritise egg yolk, liver, minced meat, and ndengu several times a week.
  • No added salt or sugar before 12 months; herbs like dhania work well instead.
  • Introduce plain water slowly — about 20ml per meal — alongside solids.
  • Give honey only after 12 months, when the botulism risk from honey has passed.
  • Food before 1 is mostly for learning, not quantity — breastmilk or formula still does the heavy lifting.
  • Fussy about a food? Keep offering it — it can take 8–10 tries before it's accepted.

Golden rules

If baby's tummy is unsettled

  • Constipated? Mashed banana + avocado with a spoon of natural yogurt, plain wimbi porridge, and butternut can help gently.
  • Loose stools? Ripe banana, plain rice, and well-cooked carrot are gentle and binding.
  • Windy or colicky? A pinch of dhania or cumin in cooking can help; avoid too much cabbage or dried beans at once.
  • Always cook food until soft, cool before serving, and keep portions small — baby's stomach is roughly the size of their fist.
  • Use plain, unsweetened natural yogurt only — avoid flavoured or sugary versions, and introduce it gradually while watching for reactions.
  • This planner offers general, non-clinical guidance — check with your paediatrician or clinic for anything persistent, or before introducing foods if baby has a known allergy risk.
